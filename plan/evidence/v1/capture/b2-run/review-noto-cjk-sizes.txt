## https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/notosanssc/upstream_info.md (source of the Google Fonts binary)
Noto Sans SC is a regional subset of the Noto CJK mega-font family, targeting Simplified Chinese. The binary was taken from the `Sans2.004` tag at commit `523d033d`, specifically from `Sans/Variable/TTF/Subset/NotoSansSC-VF.ttf`. An 832-byte post-processing difference exists between the upstream binary and the shipped version, consistent with table rewriting during onboarding.
